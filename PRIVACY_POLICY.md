# Privacy Policy for I Don't Want Cancer

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

The "I Don't Want Cancer" application ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**No Personal Data Collection**: The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require you to create an account, and we do not have access to your identity, contact information, or location.

**Local Storage**: All intelligence briefings, search queries, and alerts are processed and stored locally on your device. This data remains under your control at all times.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App communicates with official government and health organization APIs (specifically the **openFDA API**) to retrieve the latest health-related product recalls and screening recommendations.
- When the App fetches this information, it does not send any user-identifying data to these third-party services.
- These services may receive standard technical information typical of any web request (such as your IP address), but this is not associated with any personal profile within the App.

## 3. Data Management and Deletion
You have full control over the data stored by the App. You can permanently delete all locally cached intelligence and briefings at any time by using the **"Clear intelligence memory"** feature found in the **Settings** menu.

## 4. Permissions
The App requires **Internet Access** to fetch updated health intelligence from the openFDA. It does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or other sensitive device permissions.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
